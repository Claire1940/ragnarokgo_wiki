<Section icon={Swords} title="Combat Basics and Gear Progression">

Combat in Ragnarok Go rewards preparation more than reflexes. Before pushing into harder content, make sure your fundamentals are solid.

**Core combat principles:**

- Learn enemy attack patterns before committing your cooldowns
- Keep your highest-damage skill for elite enemies and bosses
- Elemental and type advantages can multiply your effective damage
- Retreat and heal rather than dying — death usually costs time and rewards

Your gear progression should follow a simple priority order:

| Priority | Item Focus | Why It Matters |
|----------|------------|----------------|
| 1 | **Weapon** | Directly increases damage output |
| 2 | **Armor** | Survivability for harder content |
| 3 | **Accessories** | Stat boosts and utility effects |
| 4 | **Cosmetics** | Visual only, spend spare currency |

<Callout type="warning" title="Upgrade Trap">
Do not spread upgrade materials across five different weapons early on. Concentrate materials on your best-in-slot weapon for each character until it reaches its next meaningful tier.
</Callout>

A useful rule of thumb for early power budgeting:

| Activity | Power Gain | Time Cost | Priority |
|----------|-----------|-----------|----------|
| Weapon upgrade | **High** | Low | ★★★★★ |
| Daily quests | Medium | Low | ★★★★★ |
| Story progression | High | Medium | ★★★★ |
| Gear enhancement | Medium | Medium | ★★★ |
| Random gacha pulls | Unreliable | Low | ★★ |

</Section>

<Section icon={Shield} title="Resource Management: What to Save and What to Spend">

Resource management separates players who progress steadily from players who stall. Most currencies in Ragnarok Go fall into clear spend and save categories.

| Resource | Recommended Use | Avoid |
|----------|----------------|-------|
| **Premium currency** | Value banners, guaranteed picks | Random low-rate pulls |
| Stamina/Energy | Story and highest-yield farming | Low-reward repeat stages |
| Upgrade materials | Best-in-slot gear | Off-spec or placeholder items |
| Respec items | After testing builds thoroughly | Respec for minor tweaks |

<Callout type="warning" title="Banner Patience">
Hold premium currency until a banner featuring characters or gear that fit your core team appears. Pulling on random banners early is the most common resource sink for new players.
</Callout>

Additional habits worth building immediately:

- Check the in-game shop daily for discounted materials
- Complete weekly missions before they reset — they often carry the largest material payouts
- Bank surplus stamina if the game allows overflow, then spend it during bonus-rate events
- Join an active guild or community early for group content and shared rewards

</Section>
