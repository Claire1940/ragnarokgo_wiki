<Section icon={Map} title="Leveling Fundamentals: How EXP Works in Ragnarok Go">

Progression in Ragnarok Go runs on two parallel tracks: **base EXP** (character level) and **job EXP** (skill level). Both come from the same activities, but the ratios differ depending on what you do. Understanding your daily limits is the foundation of efficient leveling.

Daily monster kill quota is the main throttle on grinding. Instead of unlimited farming, you receive a fixed number of rewarded kills per day, and this quota can be extended or accelerated with consumables. Stacking quota items before a long grinding session lets you bank kills for later use.

<CardGrid cols={3}>
  <Card title="Base EXP">

    - Gates story quest progression
    - Unlocks higher-level maps and gear
  </Card>
  <Card title="Job EXP">
    - Raises **job level**
    - Unlocks and upgrades skills
    - Improves build power directly
  </Card>
  <Card title="Daily Quota">
    - Fixed **rewarded kills per day**
    - Boostable with candy items
    - Stackable up to a weekly cap
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

<Callout type="tip" title="Party Bonus Is Non-Negotiable">
Always grind in a party. Party members increase the base and job EXP your character earns per kill, and shared kills count toward everyone's quota. A full party can more than offset the slower kill speed of crowded maps.
</Callout>

<Section icon={Target} title="Leveling Route: Step-by-Step from Level 1">

The fastest route through the early and mid game follows a clear sequence. Follow these steps in order and you will minimize wasted quota and dead time.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Days 1-3: Push Story Quests">
    Clear story quests nonstop until your current level blocks further progression. Early quests shower you with gear, consumables, and unlock materials that make every later stage easier.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Save Remaining Quests">
    Once quests are level-locked, stop submitting them. Story and sub quest EXP scales with the server level, so submitting them right after a server level increase yields significantly more EXP.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Set Up Survival for AFK Grinding">
    Equip and upgrade a healing pet such as Baphomet Jr., stock pet recovery food, and configure your character's auto-recovery device so neither you nor your pet dies during long grind sessions.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Burn Daily Quota in a Party">
    Form a full party, use a monster annihilation buff, and burn your daily kill quota on the best spot for your level bracket. Save dance party and similar commission completions for server level ups.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Pivot to Card Farming at Level 52+">
    From the mid game onward, choose grinding spots based on loot as much as EXP. Target cards your class needs, or high-value cards you can sell on the auction house.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="Do Not Waste Early Quest EXP">
Submitting a stockpile of story quests before the server level increases is the single most common EXP mistake. Players who save quests consistently hit level caps days ahead of those who submit early.
</Callout>

</Section>
