<Section icon={Ship} title="What Is Amatsu in Ragnarok Go?">

Amatsu is the Japanese-inspired map region in Ragnarok Go, modeled after the classic Amatsu update from the original Ragnarok Online. It features cherry-blossom scenery, ninja-themed monsters, and one of the trickier dungeons in the mid-game progression path: the Amatsu Dungeon, home of the famous Tatami Maze.

Reaching Amatsu is not as simple as walking there. The region is separated from the main Rune-Midgarts continent, so you need to complete a short quest chain and take a ship from Alberta before you can set foot on its shores.

**Why players travel to Amatsu:**

| Reason | Details | Recommended Level |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Amatsu Dungeon | Tatami Maze floors, unique boss encounters | Base 70+ |
| Quest rewards | Zeny, EXP, and unlock of dungeon entry | Base 60+ |
| Farming spots | Karakasa, Shinoobi, Tengus monsters | Base 65+ |
| Story content | Regional lore tied to the Kunlun questline | Base 60+ |

<Callout type="tip" title="Preparation First">
Stock up on **Fly Wings** and healing items before departing Alberta. Amatsu's dungeon is far from any major vendor hub, and returning mid-run wastes valuable farming time.
</Callout>

<Section icon={Compass} title="Prerequisites Before You Sail">

Before you can travel to Amatsu, your character needs to meet a few baseline requirements. These are consistent across the Ragnarok Go progression path and mirror the requirements of the classic continent unlock system.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Reach Base Level 60">
    The Alberta quest NPCs will not offer the Amatsu journey to low-level characters. Grind in Prontera-area fields or Payon Dungeon until you hit **Base Level 60**.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Progress the Main Story">
    Some regional unlocks in Ragnarok Go are gated behind main story milestones. Make sure you have completed the chapters up to and including the Alberta port storyline.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Save Enough Zeny">
    The voyage and early quest steps involve small zeny costs. Bring at least 20,000 zeny as a comfortable buffer.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Bring Combat-Ready Gear">
    Amatsu field monsters hit harder than mid-continent mobs. Upgrade your weapon to at least +5 and carry elemental coverage if your class allows it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="Quest Gate">
If the Alberta NPC does not give you the travel dialogue, your **main story progress** is likely the blocker, not your level. Return to unfinished chapter quests in your quest log first.
</Callout>

<Section icon={Map} title="Step-by-Step: How to Go to Amatsu">

Once you meet the prerequisites, the trip itself is straightforward. Follow this route to unlock Amatsu as a permanent destination.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Travel to Alberta">
    Head to the port city of Alberta, located on the southeastern coast of the Rune-Midgarts map. Use Kafra teleport services or walk from Prontera via Payon.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Find the Sailor NPC at the Docks">
    Go to the southern docks and speak with the **Sailor** handling continental voyages. He coordinates travel to Amatsu and Kunlun.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Accept the Voyage Request">
    The Sailor asks you to run a delivery errand confirming the route. Accept the request; it typically involves talking to two or three NPCs within Alberta itself.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Board the Ship">
    Return to the Sailor after the errand. Pay the voyage fee and board the ship. A short cutscene plays and you arrive at the **Amatsu port**.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Register the Destination">
    Once in Amatsu, speak with the local Kafra or teleport NPC to register Amatsu as a saved destination. After this, travel between Alberta and Amatsu is unlocked permanently.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Travel summary:**

| Step | Location | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Alberta docks | Speak with Sailor |
| 2 | Alberta city | Complete delivery errand |
| 3 | Alberta docks | Pay fee, board ship |
| 4 | Amatsu port | Arrive, save at Kafra |
| 5 | Amatsu town | Unlock dungeon quests |

<Callout type="success" title="Unlock Confirmed">
After registering with the Amatsu Kafra, you can teleport directly between the continent and Amatsu at any time. No repeat voyage fees are needed on the standard route.
</Callout>

Many players waste their first Amatsu trip by skipping preparation. These are the most frequent errors:
