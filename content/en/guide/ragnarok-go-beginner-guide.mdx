<Section icon={Target} title="Leveling and Power Progression">

Leveling in Ragnarok GO rewards structure, not raw hours. Follow this sequence to keep your character ahead of the content curve.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Push the Main Story">
    Complete story chapters until progression slows or a power wall appears. Story quests give the best early EXP and unlock new maps and systems.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Check Your Power Level">
    Before grinding, compare your character's combat rating to the recommended rating of the next quest. If you're well below target, farm gear before retrying.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Farm Targeted Monsters">
    Pick monsters that drop the specific cards or crafting materials you need next — not just the highest-EXP target. Efficient farming is goal-driven.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Upgrade Weapon and Gear">
    Keep your weapon within a few levels of your character. Weapon upgrades give the largest damage-per-resource return early on.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Repeat and Advance">
    Once your rating clears the wall, return to the story. Alternate between story pushes and targeted farming as new difficulty tiers unlock.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Section icon={Sparkles} title="Cards, Gear, and Zeny Management">

The card system is the heart of character building. Cards dropped by monsters can be slotted into equipment to add stats and effects, and a well-built card setup often matters more than raw gear level.

**Card priority rules:**
- Cards that boost your **main damage stat** come first.
- % based bonuses outperform flat stat cards as your level grows.
- Never sell or discard cards you haven't identified — rare drops are expensive to re-obtain.
- Keep duplicates of valuable cards; future upgrades and alt characters may need them.

| Resource | Spend Freely On | Save For | Avoid Wasting On |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Zeny | Potions, basic repairs | Weapon upgrades, card slots | Cosmetic purchases early |
| Upgrade materials | Main weapon | High-tier weapon crafts | Off-class gear |
| Skill points | Core damage skills | Build-defining passives | Low-value filler skills |
| Cards | Main damage stat slots | Rarity upgrades | Duplicate random slots |

<Callout type="warning" title="The Upgrade Resource Trap">
It is tempting to upgrade every weapon you find. Instead, invest in your primary weapon type and only branch out after your main character is comfortably equipped. Materials are scarce early, and spreading them thin is the single most common beginner mistake.
</Callout>

Zeny follows the same logic: save for gear progression in the early weeks. Cosmetic and convenience purchases can wait until your income comfortably covers upgrade costs.

</Section>
