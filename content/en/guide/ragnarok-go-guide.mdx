<Section icon={Target} title="Leveling Route: Step-by-Step Progression">

Efficient leveling in Ragnarak GO follows a predictable loop: story quests for unlocks, then targeted grinding on monsters slightly below your level. Fighting monsters far above your level is usually inefficient because clear speed drops sharply.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Clear the Main Story First">
    Push the main storyline as far as possible before heavy grinding. Story quests unlock maps, systems, and features, and often reward gear good enough for early content. Do not stop to farm materials you do not need yet.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Grind Level-Appropriate Mobs">
    Once story progress stalls, farm monsters within a few levels of your character. Fast kills beat slow kills on higher-level monsters — EXP per hour is what matters, not EXP per kill.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Complete All Dailies">
    Daily quests, attendance rewards, and limited-time events are the most reliable source of free upgrade currency. Log in every day even if you only have ten minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Upgrade Your Main Weapon">
    Concentrate enhancement materials on your main weapon, then armor. A well-upgraded weapon lets you farm harder maps, which feeds the rest of your progression.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Add Secondary Characters Later">
    Only invest in a second character once your main one can comfortably clear your target farming content. This keeps your account power curve smooth.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="Avoid This Common Trap">
Do not enhance every piece of gear you find. Enhancement materials are scarce in the early and mid game, and spreading them across multiple characters or weapons is the fastest way to stall your progression.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Swords} title="Gear and Upgrade Priorities">

Gear upgrading in Ragnarok GO uses a tiered enhancement system, and knowing what to upgrade first saves weeks of wasted farming. Weapon attack translates directly into clear speed, which translates into more loot per hour across every activity in the game.

| Upgrade Target | Priority | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main weapon | **Highest** | Direct damage increase, faster farming |
| Armor (defense) | High | Survivability on harder maps |
| Accessories | Medium | Stat bonuses, situational value |
| Secondary weapons | Low | Only after main weapon is well invested |
| Alt character gear | Lowest | Only when main character is farming target content |

<Callout type="info" title="Enhancement Risk Management">
Before each major upgrade, check whether materials are better saved for a milestone level. Many enhancement systems have significant cost jumps at set levels, and planning around them prevents wasted resources.
</Callout>

A few practical rules keep your upgrade path efficient:

- Upgrade one item at a time rather than leveling several items evenly
- Replace gear before heavily upgrading low-tier equipment you will outgrow quickly
- Save premium enhancement materials for endgame-tier gear
- Sell or dismantle gear you will never use instead of hoarding inventory

</Section>

<Section icon={Sparkles} title="Farming and Currency Efficiency">

Farming efficiently is about matching your character's power to the right content. The best farming spot is the one where you can kill quickly and continuously without downtime, not the highest-level map available.

<CardGrid cols={3}>
  <Card title="EXP Farming">
    - Dense mob maps with fast respawns
    - AoE classes clear packs fastest
    - Pair with EXP boost events when available
  </Card>
  <Card title="Material Farming">
    - Target monsters that drop your current upgrade needs
    - Check drop tables before committing to a map
    - Farm in focused sessions, not idle sessions
  </Card>
  <Card title="Currency Farming">
    - Daily quests give stable income
    - Vendor and sell surplus loot consistently
    - Spend currency on permanent upgrades first
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

<Callout type="tip" title="Track Your Gains">
Spend ten minutes timing your kills per minute on two or three candidate maps. Directly comparing EXP and loot per hour between maps is more reliable than following generic "best spot" recommendations, because your clear speed is unique to your build.
</Callout>
