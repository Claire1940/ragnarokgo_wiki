<Section icon={Map} title="Understanding Where Comodo Fits in Ragnarok GO">

Comodo is one of the classic Ragnarok destination towns — a beachside city on the southwestern coast of the Rune-Midgard continent, famous in the Ragnarok IP for its dancer and bard culture, casino, and surrounding high-level hunting grounds. In Ragnarok GO, regions open progressively as you push the main story, so reaching Comodo is a mid-game milestone rather than an early-game errand.

Because Ragnarok GO is an idle RPG built around auto-battle and offline progression, travel works differently from a classic MMORPG. You generally advance through stage-based field zones rather than freely walking a continent, and new towns unlock as story progression and combat power allow.
