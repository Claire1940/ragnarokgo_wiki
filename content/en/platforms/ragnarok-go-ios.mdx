<Section icon={Target} title="iOS Setup: Step-by-Step Installation">

Getting started on iOS takes just a few minutes if you follow the correct order. The most common mistake new players make is skipping account binding, which locks progress to a single device.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Check Device Compatibility">
    Verify your iPhone runs a recent iOS version and has at least 3 GB of RAM. iPhone 8 and older devices may struggle with large field battles. Free up at least 4 GB of storage before downloading.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Download from the App Store">
    Search "Ragnarok Go" in the App Store and confirm the publisher is Gravity before tapping Get. The initial download includes a patch, so stay on Wi-Fi to avoid data charges.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Create and Bind Your Account">
    After the intro, create a character and immediately bind to a Gravity account or Apple sign-in. This protects progress and enables device transfers.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Optimize Graphics Settings">
    Open Settings and reduce shadow quality and effect density on older devices. Keep frame rate at 60 if your device supports it for smoother skill cancels.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Start the Main Quest Line">
    Follow the main quests to unlock auto-battle, cards, and party features, which dramatically speed up early leveling.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="Storage Management">
Ragnarok Go receives frequent content patches. Keep roughly 2 GB of free space after installation so updates do not fail mid-download, which can corrupt game data on iOS.
</Callout>

</Section>

Performance on iOS depends heavily on device generation and settings balance. Use this reference table to match your iPhone tier to recommended settings:

| Device Tier | Examples | Graphics Preset | Frame Rate | Expected Experience |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| High-end | iPhone 13 Pro and newer | High | 60 fps | ★★★★★ |

| Older | iPhone X, XR, SE | Low-Medium | 30–60 fps | ★★★ |
| iPad | Most modern iPads | High | 60 fps | ★★★★★ |

Additional optimization tips:

- **Close background apps** before boss raids to free RAM.
- **Enable Low Power Mode off** while playing — it caps frame rate on iOS.
- **Reduce notification interruptions** using Focus mode during long grinding sessions.
- **Play on Wi-Fi** for guild war content to reduce latency spikes.

<Callout type="success" title="Battery Tip">
Turning screen brightness down to about 50% and using effect quality Medium extends battery life during long grinding sessions by a noticeable margin without hurting gameplay clarity.
</Callout>
