Mobile controls change how you approach combat compared to PC Ragnarok titles. These adjustments help new Android players settle in quickly.

| Topic | Mobile Tip | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Combat | Use auto-target for normal attacks, manual for skills | Balances convenience and control |
| Camera | Pinch to zoom during mob fights | Better spatial awareness in crowds |
| Skills | Place key skills on the main hotbar | Faster reaction in boss fights |
| Quests | Enable quest auto-pathing | Saves travel time on small screens |
| Battery | Lower graphics to medium | Longer sessions without overheating |
