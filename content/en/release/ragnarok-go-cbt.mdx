<Section icon={Map} title="What Is the Ragnarok: GO CBT?">

The **Ragnarok: GO CBT** (Closed Beta Test) refers to the limited-access testing phases that preceded the full launch of Ragnarok: GO, a Gravity-authorized pixel-art idle RPG for mobile. Closed beta tests for mobile games typically work like this: the publisher opens a limited number of server slots, invites registered players, and uses the phase to stress-test servers, balance progression rates, and gather feedback before the public release.

In Ragnarok: GO's case, the game progressed from pre-registration through regional testing into a commercial launch covering **11 Southeast Asian regions**, as reported by [Inven Global](https://labs.invenglobal.com/articles/27878/ragnarok-go-launches-in-11-southeast-asian-regions) and [O Santuario Geek](https://www.osantuariogeek.com.br/en/mobile/ragnarok-go-pre-registration-pixel-art-idle-rpg-southeast-asia). That means most players searching for a CBT today are really looking for one of two things: future test phases (new regions or content betas) or how to access the already-released game.

| Beta Phase | Typical Access Method | What You Keep After Launch |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-registration | Sign up via official site or app stores | Launch rewards, free draws |
| Closed Beta Test (CBT) | Invite or limited slots via official channels | Usually wiped; sometimes CBT gift codes |
| Open Beta / Soft Launch | Direct download in supported regions | Progress usually retained |
| Full Launch | App Store / Google Play / official APK | All progress retained |

<Callout type="warning" title="Watch Out for Fake CBT Links">
Never download a "CBT client" from unverified APK mirrors. During popular betas, scam sites distribute malware under beta-access pretenses. Only use the official website, the official App Store listing, or the Google Play listing shared by Gravity's verified channels.
</Callout>
