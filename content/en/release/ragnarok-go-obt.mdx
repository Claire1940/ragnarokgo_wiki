<Section icon={Sparkles} title="Gear, Crafting, and Equipment Progression">

Equipment is the real progression currency of the OBT. Between crafting, refining, and the Soul Echo system, your combat power comes from several parallel tracks that all need attention.

| Progression Track | What It Does | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| Crafting | Produces gear with a chance at orange/red quality bonus stats | High |
| Refining | Upgrades existing equipment power incrementally | High |
| Soul Echo | Five equip slots unlocking resonance effects every five levels | Medium |
| Pet Slots | One primary, three utility, five assist pets add flat stats | Medium |
| Talents | Passive tree plus job talents for build customization | High |

Refining carries risk as levels climb, so concentrate early refining resources on your weapon before touching armor. A refined weapon accelerates every farming activity, which then funds the rest of your gear.

<Callout type="tip" title="Crafting Discipline">
Craft in bursts when you have materials stockpiled, not one piece at a time. Batch crafting gives you more chances at orange and red quality rolls, and duplicates can be sold through your stall to recover costs.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info" title="Soul Echo Synergy">
Every five Echo levels unlock a random resonance, and stacked resonances can activate a set. Since resonance rolls are random, plan to level multiple Echo pieces evenly rather than maxing one — a completed resonance set generally outperforms a single overleveled piece.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Trophy} title="Dungeons, PvP, and Economy Checklist">

Endgame activities in the **Ragnarok GO OBT** split into instanced dungeons, PvP modes, and the player-driven economy. Large-party dungeons like god-realm raids drop equipment and Echo gear, while mid-size party dungeons feed crafting materials. PvP modes — large team battles and preset-build survivor arenas — reward consistent participants.

Your personal stall is the economy backbone. One key limitation to remember: stall pricing is locked to copper coins, so high-value trades require price planning around the game's three-currency structure.

<Callout type="warning" title="Currency Awareness">
Learn the three-currency split early — basic consumables use one currency, player trading and auctions use Zeny, and the premium-tier currency sits above both. Track what the item shop sells for each currency and which purchases actually affect progression before spending anything.
</Callout>
