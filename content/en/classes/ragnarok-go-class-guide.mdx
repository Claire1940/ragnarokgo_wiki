<Section icon={Map} title="How Classes Work in Ragnarok Go">

Ragnarok Go keeps the philosophy that made the original Ragnarok Online legendary: your class is a foundation, not a cage. Two players can pick the same class and end up with completely different characters because stats, equipment, cards, and skill interactions all reshape how a build performs. Before choosing, understand the three layers of character power:

| Layer | What It Controls | How Flexible Is It? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Base Class | Skill tree and core identity | Fixed once chosen |
| Stats | Scaling of attacks, casts, and sustain | Adjustable as you level |
| Cards & Gear | Elemental damage, crit, DoT, summons | Fully swappable anytime |

<Callout type="tip" title="Build Freedom">
The builds the game presents are examples, not rules. Once you understand stats, cards, and gear interactions, you can mix systems, break the intended builds, and redefine your class entirely.
</Callout>

<Section icon={Target} title="Choosing Your Class: Step-by-Step">

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Identify Your Preferred Combat Range">
    Decide upfront whether you enjoy melee (Knight, Blacksmith, Battle Priest), mid-range burst (Assassin, Gunslinger), or ranged casting and shooting (Wizard, Archer).
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Test the Intro Build">
    Play each class's default build for the first few hours. Note which one clears early instances comfortably — the ADL Archer is widely considered the smoothest starter.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Pick a Build Direction">
    Choose between burst damage, sustained damage over time, area control, or summon support. Each class has at least one option in every category.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Align Cards and Gear">
    Collect cards that reinforce your chosen mechanic — poison cards for DoT Assassins, crit cards for auto-attack builds, elemental cards for Wizards.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Reevaluate at Mid-Game">
    Slow starters like Trapper and Poison builds begin paying off once card slots and gear scaling come online. Adjust before committing endgame resources.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="success" title="Solo Player Recommendation">
If you mostly play solo, the ADL Hunter Archer or Pure Physical Blacksmith offer the most consistent clears without relying on party support or complex setups.
</Callout>
