<Section icon={Trophy} title="Party Strategy for Boss Fights">

World bosses and high-level field monsters are where multiplayer coordination pays off the most. Damage contribution mechanics mean every party member earns rewards, but unprepared groups waste attempts.

| Strategy Element | What to Do | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Element Check | **Match elemental advantage** to the boss | Boosts damage significantly |
| Role Assignment | Assign tank, DPS, and support upfront | Prevents chaos mid-fight |
| Consumables | Bring potions and buff items | Sustains long encounters |
| Timing | Coordinate burst skills together | Maximizes contribution score |
| Positioning | Spread out for AoE-heavy bosses | Reduces party-wide damage |

<Callout type="tip" title="Contribution Over Kills">
In group boss encounters, rewards are usually tied to your damage and support contribution rather than the killing blow. Even support players who heal and buff effectively earn solid rewards.
</Callout>
