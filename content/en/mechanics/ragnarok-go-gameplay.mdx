<Section icon={Map} title="Ragnarok: GO Gameplay Overview">

Ragnarok: GO is a Gravity-authorized pixel-art idle RPG for mobile, blending the classic Ragnarok job system with modern auto-battle convenience. Launched across 11 Southeast Asian regions, it supports auto-combat, offline growth, pet collecting, job advancement, team dungeons, and real-time chat in more than 10 languages.

The game is designed around **progression without constant attention**: your character keeps farming while you are offline, and active playtime is spent pushing story gates, upgrading gear, and clearing limited-time content.

| Gameplay System | What It Does | Player Priority |
|---|---|---|
| Auto-Battle | Characters fight and farm automatically | Foundational for all content |
| Offline Progression | Resources accumulate 24/7 while logged out | Collect before storage caps |
| Job System | Advance through classic Ragnarok class routes | High, unlocks core skills |
| Pets & Partners | Provide stats, skills, and clear support | Medium-high, scale with investment |
| Team Dungeons | Group content with better resources | High at mid-game |
| Events & Free Draws | Limited-time rewards and recruitment | High, time-sensitive |

<Callout type="tip" title="Core Loop">
Spend active time pushing the main story and spending limited daily attempts; let offline farming handle the resource grind. Never leave idle rewards sitting at the storage cap.

<Section icon={Target} title="Daily Progression Step-by-Step">

An efficient daily loop is what separates fast accounts from stalled ones. Follow this order every day before spending any stamina or enhancement materials.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Collect Idle Rewards First">
  Log in and claim all accumulated offline earnings before they hit the storage limit. These rewards fund every other upgrade in the game.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Clear Limited Attempts">
  Run daily-limited content: resource stages, limited dungeons, and event challenges. These attempts do not carry over, so they are always the highest-priority activity.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Complete Daily Missions and Events">
  Finish daily missions, sign-in calendars, and event objectives. Prioritize time-limited event missions before ordinary farming.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Upgrade the Current Bottleneck">
  Identify the exact stat or damage requirement blocking your next story stage, then upgrade only the equipment slot or pet that addresses it.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Reset for Offline Farming">
  Before logging out, park your character in the highest stable farming area your build can clear without defeats.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="success" title="Efficiency Rule">
Complete reset-based and limited content first, then manual farming. Any stamina left over goes to the resource stage matching your current bottleneck.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Shield} title="Equipment, Pets, and Build Priorities">

Resources are scarce, and spreading them evenly across every slot is the most common early-game mistake. Match your upgrades to the content you actually play.

| Content Type | Upgrade Priority | Pet/Partner Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Story stages | Weapon, main damage gear, survival pieces | Consistent clear support |
| Boss encounters | Weapon, single-target and crit bonuses | Sustained damage, survivability |
| Team dungeons | Role-defining weapon, core skills | Team utility matching your role |
| Idle farming | Clear-speed weapon, attack speed, sustain | Stability, reduced downtime |

<Section icon={Sparkles} title="Events, Free Draws, and Resources">

Event rewards in Ragnarok: GO are spread across login pages, mission panels, event shops, and limited dungeons, which makes them easy to miss. Launch-era accounts can access up to **4,000 new player draws**, so claiming beginner resources before spending premium currency is essential.

- **Sign-in calendars**: claim daily; check whether missed days are recoverable.
- **Growth campaigns**: hit listed level and story milestones early.
- **Limited dungeons**: spend event attempts before ordinary farming.
- **Free draws**: use within each reset period before they expire.
- **Exchange shops**: buy the hardest-to-farm progression materials first.

<Callout type="info" title="Official Channels">
Codes and event announcements come from the official website, the official Facebook page, and the official Discord. Verify any code through these channels before redeeming, since campaign windows vary by event.
