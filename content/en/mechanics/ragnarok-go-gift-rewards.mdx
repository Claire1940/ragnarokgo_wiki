<Section icon={Map} title="How to Redeem Codes Step-by-Step">

Redemption happens entirely inside the game client. You do not need to visit an external website or link an account, which keeps the process fast and safe.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Create Your Character">
    Pick a server (Prontera 1–10 at launch; choose one with a green population indicator for smoother matchmaking), set your character name, and finish the opening tutorial.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Open Your Profile">
    Tap your character portrait, usually in the top corner of the main HUD. This opens the profile panel containing your stats, equipment, and account options.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Find the Redemption Code Field">
    Inside the profile menu, locate the "Redemption Code" (or equivalent) entry field. It sits alongside account and settings options.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Enter Each Code Exactly">
    Type the code carefully — capitalization matters. Confirm each entry one at a time rather than batching, so you can see which rewards land.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Collect Rewards from Your Inbox">
    Redeemed items are typically delivered to your in-game mailbox. Open the mail, claim everything, then check your backpack to confirm the items arrived.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="tip" title="Redeem Before Summoning">
