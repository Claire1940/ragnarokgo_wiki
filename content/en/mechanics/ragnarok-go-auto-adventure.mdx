Auto adventure differs from manual play in one key way: efficiency comes from **preparation, not reaction**. Since you are not controlling the character moment to moment, your results depend entirely on the map you choose, the gear you equip, and the settings you configure before walking away.

<Callout type="tip" title="Core Principle">
Think of auto adventure as a farming machine. Your job is not to play better in the moment — it is to build a setup that plays well on its own for hours at a time.

<Section icon={Map} title="Choosing the Right Farming Map">

Map choice is the single biggest factor in auto adventure efficiency. A map that is too hard means constant deaths and potion drain; a map that is too easy wastes your character's damage potential.

| Map Type | Monster Difficulty | EXP Rate | Potion Use | Best For |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Low-level fields | Easy, 1-2 hits to kill | Low | Minimal | Early levels, new accounts |
| Mid-tier zones | Moderate, 3-5 hits | Medium | Moderate | Steady progression, Zeny farming |
| High-tier zones | Hard, slow clears | High | Heavy | Well-geared characters |
| Card/loot farming spots | Varies | Medium | Varies | Targeting rare drops |

Follow these guidelines when selecting a stage:

- **Survival first**: your character should finish each fight above 50% HP without emergency items
- **Kill speed second**: monsters should die within a few skill rotations
- **Loot value third**: prioritize maps that drop gear or cards you actually need
- **Density matters**: maps with tightly packed spawn points reduce travel downtime between kills

<Callout type="warning" title="Avoid Death Loops">
If your character dies repeatedly on auto, the respawn downtime can erase hours of farming gains. Drop down one difficulty tier immediately — slightly lower EXP per kill with zero deaths almost always outperforms risky high-tier maps.
</Callout>

<Section icon={Target} title="Auto Adventure Setup: Step-by-Step">

Configuring your character properly takes only a few minutes and pays off across every future session.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Open the Auto Settings Menu">
    Access the automation panel from the main HUD. This is where you define which skills the AI uses and in what order during auto adventure.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Set Your Skill Rotation">
    Place your strongest spam-friendly skill first. Long-cooldown burst skills should be lower priority so they fire only when available, while your reliable filler attack handles the bulk of the clearing.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Configure Auto-Potions">
    Set HP and SP potion thresholds — triggering around 60-70% HP is a common safe value. Confirm the potion tier matches your current bank so the system does not run dry mid-session.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Select the Farming Map">
    Choose your stage based on the survival and kill-speed criteria above. Lock the map so the AI does not wander into adjacent, harder zones.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Test Before Going Idle">
    Watch one full clear cycle manually. If the character survives comfortably and clears quickly, you are safe to leave it running or log off for offline rewards.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="success" title="Setup Checklist Passed">
A properly configured character should clear a full monster respawn cycle without dropping below half HP and without burning through more than a few potions. If both conditions hold, your setup is ready for long sessions.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Swords} title="Upgrade Priority and Resource Management">

Idle income flows in constantly, but spending it wisely is what separates fast progress from stagnation.

| Priority | Upgrade Target | Why It Matters | Recommended Timing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Weapon refinement | Faster kills = more EXP and loot per hour | As soon as affordable |
| 2 | Armor refinement | Reduces potion consumption and death risk | Before pushing harder maps |
| 3 | Skill levels | Boosts auto-rotation damage output | When Zeny surplus allows |
| 4 | Auto-consumable stock | Keeps sessions running unattended | Before every long session |
| 5 | Utility unlocks | Quality-of-life and convenience features | After core combat is solid |

Key resource habits:

- **Sell junk loot in bulk** during check-ins to convert inventory into upgrade currency
- **Reinvest immediately** — Zeny sitting idle in your wallet earns nothing
- **Stock potions before logging off** so overnight sessions do not stall
- **Track your strongest bottleneck** and pour resources into fixing it, rather than spreading upgrades thin

<Callout type="info" title="Offline Rewards Have Limits">
Most idle systems accumulate rewards only up to a maximum offline duration. Log in at least once or twice per day to claim accumulated rewards and restart the timer — otherwise farming hours are simply lost.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Trophy} title="Daily Auto Adventure Routine and Goals">

**Suggested daily loop:**

2. Sell junk loot and restock potions
3. Reinvest currency into your current upgrade priority
4. Re-evaluate your farming map — move up if clears feel trivial, down if potions drain fast
5. Restart auto adventure and log off
